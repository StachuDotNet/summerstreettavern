# Domain Name Recommendations for Summer Street Tavern

## Executive Summary

After researching available domain extensions and best practices for bar/tavern businesses, here are the recommended domain options for Summer Street Tavern in Barre, VT.

## Key Findings

- **.tavern TLD does NOT exist** - This top-level domain is not available
- **.vt and .vermont TLDs exist but are NOT publicly available** - Vermont state chose not to purchase these for $185k
- **.bar TLD is perfect for this business** - Established in 2014 specifically for bars, pubs, nightclubs, and taverns
- **.pub TLD also exists** - Can represent "pub" (bar/tavern) or "publication"

## Top Recommendations

### Tier 1: Best Options (Short & Memorable)

1. **summerst.bar** ⭐ HIGHLY RECOMMENDED
   - Very short and memorable
   - Plays on "Summer Street"
   - .bar extension is perfect for taverns
   - Easy to say verbally and remember

2. **summerstreet.bar**
   - Clear and descriptive
   - Uses the full street name
   - Professional and easy to remember
   - .bar immediately identifies the business type

3. **sst.bar** or **sstvt.bar**
   - Ultra-short (great for marketing materials)
   - "SST" = Summer Street Tavern
   - "VT" adds Vermont identity
   - Easy to type on mobile

### Tier 2: Traditional Options

4. **summerstreettavern.com**
   - Traditional .com extension
   - Full business name
   - PRO: Most recognizable extension
   - CON: Long (19 characters)
   - CON: Easy to mistype

5. **summerstbarre.com** or **summerstbarrevi.com**
   - Includes location (Barre)
   - Shorter than full name
   - Good for local SEO

6. **sstvt.com**
   - Very short abbreviation
   - Includes VT for Vermont
   - Easy to remember and type

### Tier 3: Alternative Options

7. **summerstreet.pub**
   - Uses .pub extension (tavern/bar related)
   - Shorter than .com option

8. **barrevt.bar** or **barre.bar**
   - Location-focused
   - Could work if the business wants to emphasize Barre

## Domain Extension Pricing

Based on research:

- **.bar domains**: $2.23 - $3.48/year (registration), ~$53/year (renewal at some registrars)
- **.pub domains**: Similar pricing to .bar
- **.com domains**: $10-15/year typically

## Recommendations Summary

**#1 Choice: summerst.bar** or **summerstreet.bar**

These options:
- Are short and memorable
- Use the .bar extension which immediately identifies the business
- Leverage the street name (local recognition)
- Are modern and distinctive
- Likely available (need to verify)

**#2 Choice: sstvt.bar**

If you want the absolute shortest option with Vermont identity.

**Fallback: summerstreettavern.com**

If .bar domains don't appeal or aren't available, go traditional with .com.
